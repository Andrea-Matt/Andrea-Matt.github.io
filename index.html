Andrea Matticchio

PhD student in Linguistics, UMass Amherst

I’m a PhD student in the Department of Linguistics at UMass Amherst. In fall 2026, I’m also a visiting fellow at NegLaB, Goethe University Frankfurt.

My research is on the logical properties of natural language meaning, focusing on questions & answers, presuppositions, and negation. I often collaborate with Maik Thalmann.
